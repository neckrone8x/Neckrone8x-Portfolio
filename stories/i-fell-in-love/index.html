I Fell In Love With My Best Friend

Sometimes the person you never expected becomes the person you cannot stop thinking about.

Anonymous August 25, 2026 842 reads

I never thought I would be writing something like this.

My best friend and I have known each other for years. We have always been close. We talk about almost everything, laugh about the stupidest things and have been there for each other through difficult moments.

Somewhere along the way, however, my feelings started changing.

At first I tried to ignore it. I told myself that it was just a phase and that eventually everything would go back to normal.

But it didn't.

Every time my phone lights up with a message from my best friend, I find myself smiling. Every time we spend time together, I notice things I never used to notice before.

The hardest part is that I don't know whether I should say anything.

What if they don't feel the same way?

What if telling them destroys the friendship we have spent years building?

For now, I have decided to keep it to myself. Maybe one day I'll find the courage to say what I really feel.

Until then, I guess this is the one place where I can finally admit it.
